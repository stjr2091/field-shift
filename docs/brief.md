# Brief (standing)

Updated 14 Nov 2026, from Stephen's brief to the team.

- NASA Space Apps, Field Shift challenge, team AgriCloud. Submission by Sunday 15 Nov, 23:59 EAT.
- The MVP answers one question: what do I plant, and when. No yield prediction.
- It reads rainfall and soil together for about five candidate crops with sourced requirements, then compares documented current practice with other sequences and planting windows under the same assumptions.
- Outputs: a reproducible notebook, maps, graphs and tables, a thin webapp that shows precomputed results, and a technical write-up.
- Landsat greenness is parked for now.
- Still open: the five crops, the demo area and the rules table. Checked sources so far: MAAIF maize and beans manuals, a finger millet report, and a 2022 Rakai/Nakasongola planting-date trial. Where a rule has no source, mark it unknown.
- Team: Joel has visualisation and demo, Marvin has communication. The data and rules areas are still open.

Stack and repo rules in this repo are starting suggestions; the team's baseline rules win wherever they differ, to be settled on a call. Planning stays with Stephen.
