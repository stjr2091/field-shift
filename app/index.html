Crops and rules

Partly answered Crops shown, thresholds not

Each crop needs four sourced rules: a planting rainfall threshold, a season length, a soil pH range and a temperature range. The rule table has no sourced rows yet. Three crops clear three of four and are shown. No crop has a planting rainfall threshold, so no crop is tested against the rain record.

The cut is shown rather than hidden: sorghum and groundnut have no sourced rows in the released table, so they do not appear. The table below reads results/app_data.json and computes nothing.
